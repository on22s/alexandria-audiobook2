## TTS verification follow-up

Confirmed from 160 downloaded WAVs: all expected clips exist and durations reproduce the original artifact. The same-seed W1 control is byte-identical for 32/32 clips. W4 and W16 timings give 1.93x and 3.38x elapsed-time speedups versus W1 for 32 synthetic word-mix clips; this is one A6000 run, not a general audiobook-quality result.

The memory audit found an actual reporting error. Production generation resets Torch peak counters before each sub-batch. The outer sampler therefore recorded only the last sub-batch Torch peaks. Warmup-excluded generation logs give the following allocated-memory peaks; raw measurements are preserved unchanged.

| Worker cap | Actual timed sub-batches | Saved last-sub-batch allocated peak | Maximum allocated peak in logs | Sampled peak device use |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 32×1 | 4.31 GB | 4.93 GB | 5.88 GB |
| 4 | 8×4 | 7.15 GB | 7.41 GB | 12.39 GB |
| 16 | 2×(13+3) | 6.41 GB | 13.83 GB | 23.70 GB |

The cap 16 arm never generated a full 16-item sub-batch because existing length grouping split each call into 13 and 3. The device-memory sampler and allocation peaks measure different quantities and must not be interchanged. Reserved-memory historical fields have the same counter-reset limitation. The experiment producer now accumulates the production-returned allocation peak and explicitly labels the last-sub-batch reserved peak for future runs; no TTS generation/safety behavior changes.

The captured control shell runner logs its exit code after a date substitution, which can replace it with zero. Its success messages are not accepted as verification; raw scripts are preserved as compressed archival evidence, and completion is established from independently decoded and scored artifacts. The repository shell safety test flagged that historical runner during the first release pass; it remains unchanged in the archive and is not installed as executable code.

The independent CPU recheck completed in 806.2 seconds without regenerating speech. All 160 clips decode as finite, nonzero, mono 24 kHz audio, have the recorded durations, and have no samples at the audit's clipping threshold. ASR was recomputed for 128 unique WAVs; the 32 byte-identical rerun clips reuse the identical transcript. Seven scorer controls (speaker matching) and four WER controls, including rejected cases, precede the measurements.

127 of 128 unique clip WERs match the original rounded values; W4 line 27 changed from 0.111 to 0.095238 in CPU decoding. Every reported mean WER and count over 0.2 still matches. All saved 120-character transcript prefixes reproduce. The entire transcripts, references, waveform hashes, individual scores and source hashes are retained in [the CPU result](../evidence/thunder_20260929/tts_verified_20260929_1832.json).
