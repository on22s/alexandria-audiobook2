## 2. Two-step (annotate freeform, then serialise) — BUILT, QUEUED

The user's design: stage 1 annotates with no JSON demanded, stage 2 converts.
Motivated by measurement: of 93 unanswered rows with a raw_response, **81** are
the model writing its own analysis where the JSON belongs, reaching correct
conclusions inside that prose.

- `app/two_step.py`, `freeform` codec in `app/response_codecs.py`,
  `--output-format two_step` on `generate_script.py`.
- Stage 1 runs through `call_llm_for_entries` with a codec that wraps the whole
  reply in one sentinel entry — NOT a second bare chat call, so it keeps length
  escalation, the repeat-prompt fingerprint and the attempt observer (Rule 15).
- **Stage 2 never sees the source chunk.** Only stage 1's output. Handing it the
  chunk would let it redo stage 1's job and the arms would stop being comparable.
- The gate still scores FINAL entries against the ORIGINAL chunk. Pinned by
  `test_a_stage2_drop_is_caught_by_the_gate` (rejects at 36% recall) and
  `test_empty_stage1_fails_without_calling_stage2` (exactly one LLM call).

Cost stated up front: two calls per chunk, and stage 2 adds a second place text
can vanish. It earns its place only if it turns fewer chunks into spirals.

## 3. PDNC ships 10 of 28 novels with the apostrophes stripped — FIXED

Traced from a real failure: DaisyMiller broke BOTH arms of the tail test (129
rejections, 3 adaptive splits, permanent chunk failure, no output).

**Cause is upstream.** `pdnc/data/DaisyMiller/novel_text.txt` has ZERO
apostrophes and 208 broken contractions; our `pdnc_inputs/DaisyMiller.txt` is
byte-for-byte the same size. The same novel's `quotation_info.csv` still has
its apostrophes, so PDNC lost them producing the plain text. Not our pipeline.

**Why it is not cosmetic.** `generate_script` can never pass its quality gate on
such a book: the model is told to preserve the author's text, writes "don't"
where the source says "don t", and `validate_chunk_quality` scores the
correction as MISSING content. Every retry reproduces it. Unwinnable.

**The repair** (`app/apostrophe_repair.py`, wired into `get_preprocessed_source`)
was validated against ground truth — strip apostrophes from the 18 healthy
novels, repair, compare: **22,329 of 26,681 recovered (83.7%)**, 66 false
insertions across ~7M chars. The 16.3% missed are unrecoverable by construction
(trailing possessives, closing-quote apostrophes).

Two bugs the measurement caught and reasoning did not:
- `once re-entered` matched as `once` + `re` (`\b` is satisfied by the hyphen),
  emitting `once're-entered`. **That one case was two thirds of all false
  insertions.** A `(?![-\w])` lookahead took them 108 -> 66.
- The first gate returned "healthy" on seeing ANY apostrophe, which excluded the
  worst book: **HowardsEnd has exactly one**, inside a quoted verse, against 763
  broken contractions. The gate is proportional for that reason.

**Training corpus:** 1,796 broken contractions across 728 of 3,974 rows (18.3%).
Rebuilt as `*.repaired.jsonl` **beside** the originals — the originals are the
provenance for every adapter already trained and must not be overwritten.

## 4. Base weights are NOT the cause of the Qwen3.8 regression

Two controls finished and both are negative on 383 rows each:

| arm | base | tuned | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| BF16 control (H100) | 71.0% | 70.0% | **-1.0** |
| unsloth bnb4 control (A100) | 67.4% | 65.8% | **-1.6** |

Training from proper BF16 weights does not fix it, and unsloth's pre-quantized
checkpoint reproduces the same result. Combined with template, batch shape,
precision, placement and the llama.cpp path, that is six eliminations. Both
arms used our hyperparameters and our 100%-direct-answer corpus, which is
exactly what the queued reasoning-mixture arm varies.

## 5. Corrections to claims I made during this session

Recorded because each was stated confidently before being checked.

- **"13x more gate rejections" (line format).** Wrong. Rejections landed on TWO
  DISTINCT CHUNKS IN EACH ARM; the 26-vs-2 raw counts are an artifact of
  `process_chunk_adaptively` reusing the parent chunk number for every split part.
- **"unanswered: 383 of 766".** My arithmetic, not the data — `n - available`,
  where `available` is a roster field that is 0 in some artifacts. Real counts:
  base 22 empty / tuned 39 (unsloth), base 14 / tuned 21 (BF16).
- **"The evaluations are unaffected by the apostrophe damage."** Too broad. True
  of the `distill_eval` gold set only. Measured at quote level:

  | book | quotes | damaged |
  |---|---|---|
  | SenseAndSensibility (goal 1.3 held-out) | 1,085 | 3.4% |
  | NorthangerAbbey (goal 1.3 held-out) | 842 | 0.4% |
  | Persuasion / MansfieldPark / Emma | 3,238 | **0%** |
  | TheSignOfTheFour | 640 | 7.5% |
  | NightAndDay | 2,091 | **25.4%** |
  | HowardsEnd | 2,606 | **21.5%** |

  **Goal 1.3 survives** — three of five held-out books are clean, worst is 3.4%.
  The heavily damaged books sit in the exploratory PDNC pilots at 21-25%, where
  several already report differences of five or six lines in 600 — the same
  order as the damage. Those pilots are the ones to distrust.
